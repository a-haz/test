Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

The short version

QuickTools is a static website. Every tool runs entirely in your browser. We have no servers that receive your data, no user accounts, no analytics scripts and no tracking cookies. Whatever you type into a tool stays on your device.

Data we process

Tool inputs: text, JSON, invoice details, passwords and anything else you enter are processed locally by JavaScript in your browser. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party, and they disappear when you close or reload the page (except as noted below).

Local storage: the Invoice Generator offers a "Remember my details" button. If you use it, your business details, currency and tax-rate preferences are saved in your browser's localStorage on your device only, so they can be pre-filled on your next visit. You can erase this at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like virtually all web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical request data (such as IP addresses) for security and operational purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details. We do not have access to, or interest in, such logs beyond what GitHub provides to all Pages sites.

Advertising

This site currently shows no advertising. If advertising is added in the future, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what the ad provider collects and how to opt out.

Changes & contact

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date. Questions? Open an issue on the site's GitHub repository.
