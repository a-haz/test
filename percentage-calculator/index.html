The formulas

  • X% of Y = Y × X ÷ 100. Example: 15% of 200 = 200 × 0.15 = 30.
  • X as a percent of Y = X ÷ Y × 100. Example: 30 of 200 = 15%.
  • Percent change from A to B = (B − A) ÷ A × 100. Example: 50 → 65 = +30%.

FAQ

How do I quickly calculate a tip?

Use the first calculator: enter the tip rate (e.g. 18) and the bill amount. Mental shortcut: 10% is just moving the decimal point one place left; 20% is that doubled; 15% is 10% plus half of it again.

What's the difference between percentage change and percentage points?

If an interest rate rises from 4% to 5%, that's an increase of 1 percentage point, but a 25% percentage change (since 1 is a quarter of 4). News reports often blur this distinction.

Why does a 50% drop need a 100% gain to recover?

Because the base changes. Drop 100 → 50 is −50%, but recovering 50 → 100 means doubling, i.e. +100%. Percent changes are always relative to the starting value.
