How to create an invoice

  1. Fill in your business details under From and your client's under Bill To.
  2. Add a line for each product or service — quantity × rate is calculated automatically, as are subtotal, tax and total.
  3. Click Print / Save as PDF and choose "Save as PDF" as the destination to download the finished invoice.
  4. Optionally click Remember my details so your business info and currency are pre-filled next visit (stored only in your browser).

FAQ

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes — completely free, with no watermark, no sign-up and no limit on how many invoices you create.

Is my client's data safe?

Everything happens in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to any server. If you click "Remember my details", your own business info is stored in your browser's localStorage on your device only.

What should an invoice include?

At minimum: a unique invoice number, issue and due dates, your business details, the client's details, an itemized list of goods or services, tax if applicable, the total amount due, and payment instructions (which you can put in the Notes field).

How do I save it as a PDF?

Click "Print / Save as PDF", then in your browser's print dialog set the destination to Save as PDF. Every modern browser on desktop and mobile supports this.
