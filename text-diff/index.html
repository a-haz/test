FAQ

How does the comparison work?

It splits both texts into lines and computes the longest common subsequence — the standard algorithm behind tools like diff and version-control views. Lines present only in the original are marked removed; lines present only in the changed version are marked added; lines in both stay neutral.

Can it compare code?

Yes — it's line-based, so it works well for source code, config files, CSV and prose alike. For very large inputs (many thousands of lines) it may take a moment, since exact line diffing is computationally heavier than a naive comparison.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. The diff is computed entirely in your browser on a static page; nothing you paste is transmitted or stored.
