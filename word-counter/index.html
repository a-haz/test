Common length targets

  • College essay: often 250–650 words
  • Blog post: 1,000–2,000 words is a common sweet spot

FAQ

How is reading time calculated?

At an average silent reading speed of 200 words per minute. Speaking time uses 130 words per minute, a comfortable presentation pace — handy for timing speeches and video scripts.

What counts as a word?

Any run of characters separated by whitespace. Hyphenated terms like "state-of-the-art" count as one word, which matches how most word processors count.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. The counting happens in your browser as you type, and the text is never transmitted or saved. Reload the page and it's gone.
