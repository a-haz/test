FAQ

How is age calculated exactly?

It counts complete years from your birth date, then complete months, then leftover days — the same way you'd say "32 years, 4 months and 9 days". Month lengths and leap years are handled correctly by borrowing the actual number of days in the preceding month, so the result always matches the calendar.

Why might this differ from a quick "today minus birth year"?

Subtracting years alone ignores whether your birthday has happened yet this year. This tool checks the month and day, so someone born in December isn't counted a year older until December.

Can I find my age on a past or future date?

Yes — set the "Age at date" field to any date to see how old you were (or will be) then. Leave it blank to use today.

Is my birth date stored anywhere?

No. Everything is computed in your browser; nothing is transmitted or saved.
