FAQ

How is the sale price calculated?

Final price = original × (1 − discount/100). A 25% discount on $80 means you pay 75% of $80 = $60, saving $20.

How do "stacked" discounts work?

Two successive discounts don't add — they multiply. 20% off then an extra 10% off is 0.8 × 0.9 = 0.72, i.e. 28% off in total, not 30%. Apply them one at a time using this calculator to see the real price.

What's a standard tip?

It varies by country and custom. In the US, 15–20% on the pre-tax total at sit-down restaurants is typical; many places elsewhere include service or expect little to no tip. Adjust the percentage to your situation.

Is anything sent to a server?

No — it all runs in your browser on a static page.
