How the conversion works

Everything is derived from your hourly rate: weekly = hourly × hours/week, yearly = weekly × paid weeks/year, monthly = yearly ÷ 12, and daily assumes an 8-hour day. The common US shorthand "annual = hourly × 2,080" is just 40 hours × 52 weeks.

FAQ

Is this gross or net pay?

Gross — before income tax, social contributions, retirement deductions and benefits. Net (take-home) pay depends on your country, tax bracket and deductions, so no simple converter can compute it universally.

What should I put for paid weeks per year?

52 if you're salaried or get paid vacation. If you're a freelancer or hourly worker who doesn't earn while away, subtract your unpaid weeks — e.g. 48 if you take four unpaid weeks off. This is also the honest way to compare a freelance rate against a salary.

Why is the monthly figure not weekly × 4?

Because months average 4.33 weeks (52 ÷ 12). Weekly × 4 understates monthly pay by about 8%.

Freelancer tip: don't just match your old salary

Freelance rates need to cover unpaid time off, sick days, equipment, insurance and self-employment taxes — a common rule of thumb is that a contractor's hourly rate should be 1.5–2× the equivalent employee hourly rate.
