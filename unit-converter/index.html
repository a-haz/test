FAQ

How accurate are the conversions?

The factors use internationally defined exact values (for example 1 inch = 25.4 mm exactly, 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg exactly), so conversions are accurate to full floating-point precision. Temperature uses the exact Celsius/Fahrenheit/Kelvin formulas.

What's the difference between US and imperial gallons?

They're different: a US gallon is about 3.785 litres while an imperial (UK) gallon is about 4.546 litres. This tool's "gallon" is the US gallon, which is the more common default online; convert via litres if you need the imperial one.

Is anything sent to a server?

No — all conversion happens in your browser on a static page.
