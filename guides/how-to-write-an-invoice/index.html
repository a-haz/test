What every invoice must include

At a minimum, a valid invoice needs all of the following. Missing items are the most common reason payments get delayed or disputed:

  • The word "Invoice" — so it isn't mistaken for a quote or receipt.
  • A unique invoice number — for your records and the client's accounting.
  • Your details — name or business name, address, and contact info (and tax/VAT number if you're registered).
  • The client's details — who you're billing and where.
  • Issue date and due date — when it was sent and when payment is expected.
  • An itemized list — each product or service, quantity, rate, and line total.
  • Subtotal, tax, and total due — broken out clearly.
  • Payment instructions — bank details, payment link, or accepted methods.

How to number your invoices

Use a consistent, sequential scheme so nothing slips through the cracks and your bookkeeping stays clean. Common formats:

  • Sequential: INV-001, INV-002, INV-003…
  • Date-based: 2026-001, 2026-002 (resets each year).
  • Per-client: ACME-001, ACME-002 (handy if you bill a few clients regularly).

The only hard rules: never reuse a number, and never leave gaps you can't explain — both raise flags in an audit.

Choosing payment terms that get you paid

"Net 30" means payment is due 30 days after the invoice date; "Net 14" or "Due on receipt" get cash in faster. A few tactics that measurably speed up payment:

  • Shorten the window. Net 14 instead of Net 30 can cut your average wait roughly in half.
  • State the date, not just the term. "Due July 4, 2026" is harder to ignore than "Net 14".
  • Make paying effortless. Include a payment link or full bank details right on the invoice.
  • Consider a late fee. Even a small stated fee (e.g. 1.5%/month) nudges clients to prioritize you.

FAQ

Do I need to be a registered business to send an invoice?

No. Sole traders and freelancers can invoice using their own name and address. You only need a tax/VAT number on the invoice if you're registered for that tax.

What's the difference between an invoice and a receipt?

An invoice is a request for payment sent before you're paid; a receipt is proof of payment issued after. Same transaction, opposite ends.

Should I send PDF or a document file?

PDF. It looks identical on every device, can't be accidentally edited, and is the professional standard. The generator exports PDF directly.
